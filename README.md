# Survivor Fantasy Draft

A Survivor fantasy draft, played like fantasy football.

You draft the cast the way a fantasy league drafts a roster. The people you pick score through the season, and the lowest total wins. It is a private family scoreboard for seats, picks, and past seasons. There are no in-app payments.

The website, the iOS app, and the Android app live in this repo. [RELEASE.md](RELEASE.md) is the checklist for a production build, TestFlight, and Play, including the secrets and the commands. Privacy and support pages are `public/privacy.html` and `public/support.html`.
