A Survivor fantasy draft, played like fantasy football. Draft the cast, follow the season, and the lowest score wins. Private family league. No in-app payments.

## Scoring

The lowest score wins. A tie splits the pot. You only score a boot if you were a member of it. The $10 buy-in is settled between the table, outside this app. The app does not take payment.

Place names below are the labels on the sheet.

### What scores zero

- **Idols and advantages.** Zero on the sheet, unless played at final-four fire or final tribal.
- **Quits and medevacs.** Zero. A quit or medevac during tribal does not score either.
- **Edge of Extinction.** A pre-merge boot who returns is worth 0 for that boot, then scores wherever they finish.
- **Final tribal votes.** The votes themselves are worth nothing.

A first boot is worth 1. The winner is worth 22. Early boots keep a roster low, which is why the lowest total wins.

## The board

`Survivor 51` is the league in progress: 23 seats, in Fiji. Finished seasons 46–50 stay on the sheet. From `Leagues`, choose `New league` when this is not your table.

To erase a league, open it and choose `Delete league`. To erase everything saved on this device, use `Erase this device` on the `Leagues` screen.

Your seat and any leagues you create stay on this device. There is no account.
