Support

Survivor Fantasy Draft is a private scoreboard. It does not take payment. The $10 buy-in is settled between the table, outside the app.

To erase a league, open it and choose Delete league. To erase everything saved on this device, use Erase this device on the Leagues screen.

Chat

On any message that is not yours, tap Report and pick a reason. Reports are stored so they can be reviewed. Tap Block to hide that person on this device. Unblock them from the You tab.

Contact

Write from the You tab in the app, or contact github.com/tanre77.
